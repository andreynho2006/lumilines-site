Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to lumilines.app@gmail.com. In the app, Settings ▸ Send feedback opens a pre-filled email.

How do I play?

Drag from a dot to draw a line to the dot of the same color. Lines can't cross: drawing through a line cuts it. A puzzle is solved when every pair is connected and every cell is filled. The app has a short tutorial under Settings ▸ How to play.

When do new daily puzzles appear?

At midnight, your local time.

Do I need internet?

No.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings ▸ Restore purchases.
