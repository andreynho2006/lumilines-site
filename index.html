How it plays

One rule. Lots to think about.

You learn it in ten seconds. The bigger boards take a little longer.

Draw a line

Drag from a dot to its matching dot.

Never cross

Lines can bend as much as you like, but they can't cross. From Thursday, walls block some edges too.

Fill every cell

The board is solved when every square is part of a line.

A daily habit

Small on Monday. Walls by Thursday. Grand on Sunday.

Every day brings an Easy, a Medium and a Hard board. They grow until Thursday, then walls take over and thicken all the way to the Sunday Grand, the most intricate board of the week.

Questions

Good to know

How do I join the beta?

Open the beta link on your iPhone. It takes you to TestFlight, Apple's free app for testing apps before release. Install TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. Purchases in the beta use Apple's test system and never charge you.

When is it coming to the App Store?

Soon. The beta is the last step before launch, and feedback from testers shapes the final version.

What are walls?

A wall sits between two cells and blocks the edge, so a line has to go around it. Walls appear on the Medium and Hard boards from Thursday to Sunday, in the Endless Walls room, and in the Expert room.

What's the Expert room?

A Plus room in Endless with 9×9 boards, eight lines and walls. The boards are carved in the background and kept ready, so you never wait on the main board.

Does it work on iPad?

Lumilines is made for iPhone. On iPad it runs in iPhone mode for now.

What do you do with my data?

No ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your devices and in your iCloud. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I send feedback?

In the app, Settings ▸ Send feedback opens a pre-filled email. You can also write to hello@lumilines.app or use the feedback button in TestFlight.
