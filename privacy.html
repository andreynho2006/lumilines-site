Lumilines Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Lumilines does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No Lumilines account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Your game progress, results, streaks and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they also sync to your other devices through your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. We never see this data, and it is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • If you use Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • If you email us for support, we receive only what you choose to send, including the app and device details pre-filled in the email, and use it only to answer you.
  • Lumilines is suitable for all ages. It is not designed specifically for children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
